# Sample stock programmes first, graduate to mills later

Casa Slancio should start sampling with a handful of low-MOQ stock and ready-to-ship sellers, not with big mills. For the tee knit, that means Guangdong Yitong, Shaoxing Ohyeah and Eclat's Los Angeles stock programme. For the shorts woven, it means Suzhou Meidao, Suzhou Haoming, Guangzhou Yaqixin and Wupro Shishi. Alongside these, it should request swatches from bluesign-confirmed Chinese mills (Quanzhou Lianxingfa, Winsun and Fujian Unitex for knits; Wujiang Flying for wovens) and from Wujiang Canxing's spandex-free "T800" range. Those mills are the partners to grow into. The reason is arithmetic. A 300–1,000-piece run needs only about 60–300 kg of tee jersey and about 300–900 m of shorts fabric per style, depending on cut. Chinese knit mills commonly quote 300–1,000 kg **per colour**, and the best-fit shorts fabrics from manufacturers start at 500–1,000 m. So at launch volumes the realistic sources are converters, stock-service traders and programmes built for small brands. The aim is to deal with them knowingly, not to avoid them. Of the earlier quick leads, only Yitong and Ohyeah survive as first-rank samples. Laner, Molun, Ronghao, Shunshi, Baoxuan, Dake and Quanzhou Golden were downgraded or dropped after verification. Pranera is a real Tirupur company but sells polyester only. Everest and Singtex are tier-1 mills with no public small-brand route. India's edge is a 0% UAE duty under the UAE–India CEPA, which is worth only about US$35–250 per order at these volumes. Singapore has no performance-fabric mills, only trading offices. At these volumes, fixed costs drive the landed cost: testing, inspection, clearance and freight minimums. The fabric price and the 5% duty matter less. Every price, MOQ, certificate and customer name below is **seller-advertised** unless marked as checked against an independent list.

## Launch volumes fall below almost every mill's per-colour minimum

Two numbers drive the sourcing strategy: fabric volume and minimum order quantity (MOQ). By rough cutting estimates (not sourced, and to be confirmed against the Ajman factory's marker), one ~150 gsm tee uses about 0.7–1.0 m of open-width jersey, or about 0.2–0.3 kg. A pair of shorts uses about 0.6–1.2 m of 140–150 cm woven. So 1,000 tees need roughly 200–300 kg of knit, and 300 tees need roughly 60–90 kg. Split across two or three colours, that is well under the "low" knit MOQs Chinese suppliers quote: **300–500 kg per colour at the low end, and 500–1,000 kg per colour at mid-size factories** ([YXX Fabric](https://yxxfabric.com/insights/low-moq-knit-fabric-supplier-china/)). Stretch-performance fabrics often start around **800–1,000 m** ([Thai Son S.P](https://thaisonsp.com/blog/understanding-moq-in-garment-production-how-to-plan-your-order-smartly/)). One Suzhou activewear garment maker also notes that nylon- and poly-spandex fabrics "carry higher mill minimums than jersey knits", and that custom dyeing adds 8–12 days ([Renrui Textile](https://renruitextile.com/private-label-activewear-manufacturer/)).

So in practice the first run will be bought from one of four sources:
- a converter's or trader's stock fabric;
- a mill's stock colours;
- a programme designed for small brands (SwatchOn, Eclat's LA stock, Mill Direct for Polartec);
- a single colour dyed to one custom lot.

Traders are not automatically bad partners at this size. The rule is to know which kind of seller you are dealing with, and to require the name of the mill and a test report for the exact article. On Alibaba, nearly every low-MOQ (1–150 m) stretch-woven listing checked came from a "Trading Company" ([Suzhou Meidao listing](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/90-Nylon-10-Spandex-92-Polyamide_1601530915895.html)). The Alibaba profiles that also say "Manufacturer", such as Wujiang Pengnuo, start at 500–1,000 m ([Pengnuo listing](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/Recycled-Polyester-T800-T400-Poka-Stretch_1600849977498.html)).

The strongest independent quality signal available without a visit is the **bluesign System Partner list**, because it is published by a third party. **Fujian Unitex, Quanzhou Lianxingfa and Winsun Textile** appear on the September 2026 list ([bluesign PDF](https://www.bluesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/bluesign-System-Partner-list_2026.09.01.pdf)). For wovens, **Wujiang Flying Textile, Wujiang Jintang Coating** and about twenty other Wujiang and Suzhou mills appear on the April 2026 list ([bluesign PDF](https://www.bluesign.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/bluesign-System-Partner-list_2026.04.01.pdf)). None of the Alibaba-sourced knit leads is on it: Yitong, Ohyeah, Zhifa, Henly, Dake, Jiadatai and Baoxuan are all absent. That check was AI-assisted and should be repeated by hand.

## Tee knit shortlist: two stock sellers, a Taiwan benchmark and three bluesign mills

The tee calls for a soft 120–160 gsm jersey that dries quickly, resists pilling and holds its shape through 50 washes. Two constructions fit. The first is a **fine-gauge nylon/elastane or poly/elastane single jersey**: performance, quick-dry and durable. The second is a **lyocell- or modal/spandex jersey**: a softer "quiet luxury" hand, but slower drying, and pilling must be proven. Most nylon/spandex on Alibaba is legging or swimwear weight (200–300 gsm) and too compressive for a tee, so many listings that look relevant are not ([Dake specs](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/Free-Sample-87-5-Nylon-12_1601482553591.html)).

## Shorts woven shortlist: nylon-spandex for hand, T800 for longevity

The benchmark for the shorts is Toray Primeflex. It stretches without spandex, using bicomponent yarn that bonds two nylon polymers into a self-crimping "spiral" thread; Toray also offers PTT/PET versions ([Toray](https://www.sportstextiles.toray/en/primeflex/index.html)). No Chinese seller openly offers a nylon/nylon bicomponent woven. The like-for-like affordable analogue is therefore a **PTT/PET bicomponent polyester**, either genuine LYCRA® T400® or the generic Chinese "T800". LYCRA says T400's "helical coils deliver comfort stretch and superior recovery" with "no spandex shine" ([The LYCRA Company](https://www.lycra.com/en/business/search-technologies/lycra-t400-fiber)). A yarn seller puts T400 at 25–35% stretch and T800 at 35–45%, and says spandex "degrades over time" ([Yaakan](https://www.yaakan.com/stretch-yarn-ptt-pbt-t400-t800-guide/)).

Sellers contradict each other on T800's hand feel ([Redcolortex](https://www.redcolortex.com/news/industry-news/t800-vs-t400-stretch-fabric-what-makes-t800-different.html)). "T800" has no trademark owner. And Alibaba labels "T400" on plain 80 gsm pongee ([Xingmai](https://www.alibaba.com/product-detail/Ready-to-Ship-100-Polyester-T400_1601157856480.html)) and "T800" on fabrics with 8% spandex ([Rongji](https://rongjitextile.en.alibaba.com/index.html)).

The trade-off is clear:
- **90/10 nylon-spandex, 130–150 gsm, full-dull:** the softer, matte, premium hand, but it depends on elastane.
- **Spandex-free T800, 100–130 gsm:** the stronger 50-wash recovery story, though no supplier publishes 50-wash data for it.

## Low-MOQ side doors, other countries and the 2027 fair calendar

Four routes outside Alibaba fit 300–1,000 garments:

1. **SwatchOn.** Swatch books show one fabric in all its colours as ~10×10 cm cuttings. Sample yardage starts at **1 yard on every fabric**, and wholesale minimums are set per item with prices that step down by volume ([SwatchOn](https://www.swatchon.com/how-it-works)). Business accounts get **20 swatch credits** once they submit company information. Books arrive in 3–5 business days ([SwatchOn help](https://www.swatchon.com/help/articles/swatch-books-and-credits)). Its mill base is assumed to be Korea-centric, but that is unverified. Duties to the UAE are shown only at checkout.
2. **Mill Direct Textiles.** It sells genuine Polartec "without minimums". Swatches cost US$0.50, there is a 2-yard sample minimum, and international full-roll orders carry a **US$1,000 minimum**, Ex Works ([Mill Direct](https://www.milldirecttextiles.com/i/ordering-information)). This route lets Casa Slancio benchmark the real thing, but it is not a cheaper one. Whether Polartec Delta is stocked is unconfirmed.
3. **LYCRA ONE.** The free marketplace is the only route to **certified COOLMAX mills** ([LYCRA](https://www.thelycracompany.com/en/brands-coolmaxr/coolmaxr-product-sourcing)). The mill still sets the MOQ.
4. **Eclat's LA stock programme.** Covered in the tee shortlist above.

Local Deira and Dubai Textile City wholesalers list mainly fashion crepes, chiffon and rayon, not technical knits ([Make Works](https://make.works/companies/rochaan-rochas-fabrics_uae)).

Each of the other countries in scope serves a narrower purpose:
- **Singapore.** No performance-fabric mill was found. KCTEX, Cosmostar and Hunter Textile are office-based supply-chain and marketing firms ([KCTEX](https://kctexinternational.com/); [SG directory](https://sg.ltddir.com/companies/cosmostar-singapore-private-limited/)). ## Verify the licence, then make the lab prove 50 washes

Platform badges show that a company exists and has paid for checks. They do not show that it is a mill. "Gold Supplier" is a paid tier. "Verified" means a third party such as SGS or TÜV inspected the site ([Aqualora](https://www.aqualora.us/blog/vet-alibaba-suppliers-red-flags)). "Multispecialty" suppliers are almost always traders ([KOI Sourcing](https://koisourcing.com/blog/how-to-avoid-fake-alibaba-suppliers)).

The strongest free test is the **business scope on the Chinese business licence**, looked up by its Unified Social Credit Code on **gsxt.gov.cn**. A mill's scope includes 生产, 制造 or 加工 (produce, manufacture, process). A trader's is limited to 销售, 批发 or 贸易 (sales, wholesale, trade). A supplier that refuses to share its licence is a red flag ([ChineseCheck](https://chinesecheck.com/blog/identify-real-factory-vs-trading-company)). Then:
- check the address on Baidu Maps: industrial zone, or a "creative park" office;
- check the label on the SGS on-site video: "affiliate/partner company" is a warning ([KOI Sourcing](https://koisourcing.com/blog/how-to-avoid-fake-alibaba-suppliers));
- ask for a 20–30 minute **live** video walk of the floor; traders refuse or send pre-recorded clips ([Aqualora](https://www.aqualora.us/blog/vet-alibaba-suppliers-red-flags)).

For India, the equivalent checks are presumably GSTIN and IEC verification on government portals, but this was not tested here.

Certificates need the same scrutiny:
- check bluesign status on the public partner list or directory ([bluesign](https://www.bluesign.com/system-partners));
- check OEKO-TEX numbers with the OEKO-TEX Label Check, since none were checked in this research;
- check COOLMAX through LYCRA ONE;
- check TENCEL™ through Lenzing fibre certificates.

Test reports should be from the last 12–18 months, issued to that factory, and for that exact article ([Aqualora](https://www.aqualora.us/blog/vet-alibaba-suppliers-red-flags)).

On payment, **Trade Assurance** refunds only against what is written into the online order. Subjective complaints "without objective standards" are not protected, and the evidence it expects is a third-party inspection report ([Alibaba seller blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/b2b-trade/trade-assurance-complete-guide-alibaba-secure-payment)). Claims must be filed within **30 days of confirmed receipt** ([Alibaba](https://tradeassurance.alibaba.com/ta/moneybackpolicy.htm)), which is shorter than the path from sea freight through cutting in Ajman to wash testing. Write measurable specs into the order: composition, GSM ±5%, cuttable width, shrinkage, pilling and colourfastness grades. Then test **before shipment**. Suppliers' default term is 30% deposit with 70% against the bill of lading. For a first order, tie the balance to a passed inspection instead ([Novex Global](https://www.novexglobal.com/safe-payment-terms-importing-overseas-suppliers/)). Pre-shipment inspection by QIMA, SGS, BV or Intertek costs about **US$250–400 per man-day**, and one man-day covers a 300–1,000 m lot ([ChinaMakersHub](https://chinamakershub.com/journal/product-inspection-china-before-shipment)). Ask the inspector to grade rolls on the 4-point system, check shade against the lab dip, and draw bulk samples for the lab.

The 50-wash standard becomes a routine, cheap test panel. Only the extended washing is unusual.

The 50-cycle extension alone is about **US$200 per fabric/colour** at Asian rates, and the full panel about **US$120–250**. That makes it a convenient check on bulk lots once they reach Ajman.

To keep the panel affordable, test the darkest shade (worst case for colourfastness) and the lightest shade (pilling is most visible). Re-test every bulk lot for shrinkage and C06. Ask the lab how much fabric it needs; practitioners suggest 2–3 m or 3–5 sewn garments per colour for a 50-wash programme, but this is unverified.

## Fixed costs, not fabric price or duty, dominate landed cost

The UAE charges **5% customs duty on CIF value**, plus **5% import VAT on CIF + duty**. VAT is recoverable for VAT-registered businesses. A worked example: CIF AED 10,000 → duty AED 500 → VAT AED 525 ([Traddal](https://traddal.com/resources/calculate-duties-taxes-imports-united-arab-emirates)). China has no FTA with the UAE, so 5% applies; this is expected but was not confirmed against the tariff line.

The **UAE–India CEPA**, in force since **1 May 2022**, gave Indian textiles and apparel **immediate zero-duty access** ([APCO](https://apcoworldwide.com/static/dc4cc3d1a3aed2944cd84f381fd66f74/INDIA-UAE-CEPA-DECODED_April-2022-1.pdf)). Three conditions apply:
- The fabric must carry a **CEPA certificate of origin** with a unique serial number. It must be issued at or before export, or within 5 working days after.
- Chapter 60 knit fabric must meet **"CTH + 40% value added"** ([CEPA text](https://www.moet.gov.ae/documents/20121/1347101/Final+Agreement_UAE+India+CEPA.pdf)). Fabric knitted and dyed in India from yarn should normally pass, even if the nylon or elastane yarn came from China.
- Before relying on 0%, confirm the exact HS line (6006.xx for weft knits; 5407-type headings for synthetic filament wovens) in the UAE's Annex 2B schedule.

Many small Tirupur traders may never have issued a CEPA certificate, so raise it in the RFQ. Without one, 5% applies.

Freight from China to Dubai runs **US$3.1–3.9/kg by air for 100–500 kg** ([DDPChain](https://ddpchain.com/uae/)), but another April 2026 snapshot shows **US$5.57–6.30/kg** ([BSI Freight](https://www.bsifreight.com/knowledge/guides-resources/air-freight-rates-from-china-to-world)), so budget US$3–6.5/kg. LCL to Jebel Ali is **US$80–110/CBM** with a 1 CBM minimum and 15–22 days transit ([DDPLINK](https://ddplink.com/services/sea-freight-from-china/)). Fixed LCL charges at origin and destination, plus trucking to Ajman, were not sourced and can exceed the ocean freight. For loads under ~300 kg, air is often competitive. India→UAE rates were not sourced; the scenarios below use the China air band as a placeholder.

## Conclusion

The best use of the next 60 days is to treat sampling as an experiment that pits the two shorts fabrics against each other: elastane-based nylon versus spandex-free T800 polyester. Pay for one 50-wash panel per finalist rather than chasing a lower price per metre. A US$200 wash programme will tell Casa Slancio more about whether a fabric meets its standard than any supplier's badge, and it is the only objective evidence Trade Assurance will honour if a lot fails. Stock and RTS sellers carry the launch. The bluesign-confirmed mills (Lianxingfa, Winsun, Unitex, Flying) are the ones to court in parallel, through swatch requests now and meetings at Performance Days in March 2027. One colour at 300–1,000 kg or 1,000 m becomes viable once a style reorders.

On geography, India is most useful as a check on cost and duty, not as the main source of premium tees. No Indian supplier publicly offered a 120–160 gsm nylon/elastane or certified lyocell performance jersey, so the CEPA advantage applies mainly to polyester dry-fit tees and to Positex-type wovens. Taiwan's real contribution at this stage is Eclat as a quality benchmark. If its LA stock programme will ship to the UAE, which is still unconfirmed, it is the one route to Lululemon-tier knit at a start-up MOQ.
