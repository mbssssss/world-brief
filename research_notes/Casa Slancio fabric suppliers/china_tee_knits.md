# Chinese knit fabric suppliers for a premium performance/comfort T-shirt (Casa Slancio)

Research date: 25 Nov 2026. All MOQ, price, lead-time, certification and customer claims are **seller-advertised** unless I mark them as checked against a third-party list. Alibaba metrics (years, on-time delivery, response time, "assessment passed") come from the Alibaba supplier record via Firecrawl's Alexandria "alibaba" provider, observed 25 Nov 2026. Firecrawl credits ran out before the final checks, so a few items are marked as gaps.

**How I scoped it:** For Casa Slancio, 1 tee at ~150 gsm uses roughly 1.2–1.4 m² of fabric including cutting waste. That is about 0.2 kg, or about 0.7 m of 180 cm-wide fabric. So 300 tees ≈ 60–80 kg and 1,000 tees ≈ 200–270 kg (≈700–900 m) in total, split across colours. This is my own estimate, not a sourced figure. It matters because many mills quote MOQs of 360–1,000 kg **per colour**.

## Q1. Which Chinese mills make suitable knits (nylon/elastane, poly/elastane, lyocell/modal blends, cooling/quick-dry jersey), and which publicly name activewear brand customers?

### Takeaway
The strongest **mill-grade** leads came from the Functional Textiles Shanghai (by Performance Days) exhibitor list and the bluesign System Partner list, not from Alibaba. They are Fujian Unitex (Fuqing), Quanzhou Lianxingfa Knitting (Quanzhou), Winsun Textile (Wujiang/Suzhou), Henzo/Fujian Hengzhou (Changle), B-HOO (Jinjiang), Fujian Fynex (Jinjiang), Zhejiang Charming (Tongxiang) and Dongguan Jiadatai. ### Inferences
- For a premium tee at 120–160 gsm, the best-fitting constructions on offer are:
  - fine-gauge nylon/elastane or poly/elastane single jersey (Winsun's 32G–46G machines, Lianxingfa's 170 g poly/spandex, Henly's 165 gsm nylon/poly/spandex);
  - lyocell- or modal-spandex jersey (Yitong, Ohyeah, Feimei, Charming, Tonghui).
  
  Most Alibaba nylon/spandex listings are legging or swim weights (200–300 gsm). Those are too heavy and too compressive for a tee.
- Large vertical mills (Fynex, Charming, Tonghui, Unitex) probably set bulk minimums well above a 300–1,000-tee run. They are better treated as "second-stage" mills, or approached via stock/greige programmes, than as launch partners. This is not confirmed: none published MOQs.
- Brand-customer claims (B-HOO's Nike; Henzo's PUMA, Mizuno and others; Lianxingfa's ANTA and Xtep; Charming's M&S and Esprit) come only from the suppliers themselves. They should be checked against the mill's bluesign or brand-audit documentation before being relied on.

### Gaps
- I did not find any third-party confirmation (brand supplier lists, press) of the brand-customer claims above.
- The Functional Fabric Fair (Portland) exhibitor list rendered "0 Exhibitors" through the scraper. Performance Days Munich's own list and Intertextile Shanghai's list were not reached before credits ran out.
- The full FTS exhibitor list (13 pages) was not crawled, so more Chinese knit exhibitors are likely missing.

## Q2. Certifications and branded-fibre licences (OEKO-TEX, bluesign, GRS; COOLMAX/THERMOLITE; TENCEL; Polygiene/HeiQ)

### Takeaway
bluesign status can be checked against a public list. **Fujian Unitex, Quanzhou Lianxingfa and Winsun are confirmed bluesign System Partners.** None of the Alibaba-sourced leads we were given (Yitong, Ohyeah, Zhifa, Henly, Dake, Jiadatai, Baoxuan) appear on that list. COOLMAX mills are not published openly; they are found through the free LYCRA ONE Marketplace. I found no public Lenzing TENCEL licensee directory. ### Inferences
- "Ten", "Tencel" or "lyocell" on Alibaba listings should be treated as generic lyocell unless the supplier can show a Lenzing TENCEL™ fibre certificate or licence/hangtag approval for that specific fabric. Baoxuan's "Ten" labelling suggests it does not hold TENCEL™ branding rights. This is my inference.
- For the "quiet luxury" positioning, Unitex, Lianxingfa and Winsun stand out on third-party evidence: they are the only ones whose bluesign status is confirmed on an independent list.

### Gaps
- **Lenzing TENCEL licensing:** I found no public partner/licensee directory. Lenzing's licensing is handled through its e-branding service and needs an account. No supplier's TENCEL™ licence was verified.
- **COOLMAX:** I could not check whether any of these suppliers is a COOLMAX-certified mill, because that needs a LYCRA ONE login.
- **Polygiene / HeiQ:** no supplier in this set publicly named a Polygiene or HeiQ finish partnership. Not researched further.
- **OEKO-TEX:** certificate numbers were not checked with the OEKO-TEX Label Check tool. All OEKO-TEX claims above remain unverified.

### Inferences
- **Recommended sampling shortlist** (my synthesis):
  1. Guangdong Yitong: lyocell/spandex 200 gsm and modal/spandex 190 gsm via RTS at 2 m minimum. Cheap and fast, but ask for a lighter weight (150–170 gsm) and proof of where it is knitted.
  2. Shaoxing Ohyeah: 88/12 lyocell/spandex and 135 gsm nylon/spandex jersey.
  3. Quanzhou Lianxingfa: poly/spandex micro jersey, bluesign.
  4. Winsun: fine-gauge high-density jersey, bluesign.
  5. Fujian Unitex: bluesign, vertical.
  6. Henzo: Changle nylon/poly-spandex.
  7. Suzhou Henly: 165 gsm nylon/poly/spandex single jersey.
  8. Shantou Zhifa: custom lightweight nylon/spandex jersey.
- **Price context:** advertised prices for relevant constructions range from about US$2–6/kg for poly or nylon blends and about US$3–8.5 per metre or per kg for lyocell/modal blends. At ~0.2 kg per tee, fabric cost would be roughly US$0.5–1.7 per tee before freight and testing. That is far below a Polartec-branded fabric. This is my calculation from advertised prices; the Polartec Delta price itself was not researched.
- **MOQ fit:** stock/RTS programmes (Yitong at 2 m, Jiazhoutai and Jiadatai at 1 yd, Zhifa at 1 m on some items) fit a 300–1,000-tee launch. Custom-dye MOQs (Baoxuan 360 kg, Feimei 1,000 kg, Golden Nonwoven 1,000 m, Zhifa 250 m–500 kg) cover the whole run in one or two colours at most.
- **Durability requirement:** the 50-wash, low-pilling, colourfastness requirement is not evidenced by any supplier's published test data. Lab reports should be requested (e.g. ISO 12945 pilling, ISO 105-C06 wash fastness, ISO 6330 dimensional change), and in-house wash tests run on samples.
