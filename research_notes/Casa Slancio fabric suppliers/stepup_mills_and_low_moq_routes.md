# Value step-up performance fabric mills (Taiwan, Korea, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Turkey) and low-MOQ sourcing routes for Casa Slancio

Research date: 2026-11-25. Scope excludes China/India (researched separately). Research budget was ~17 tool calls, so mill-level depth is uneven; MOQs/prices are only reported where a source states them. Items marked UNVERIFIED come from snippets, self-published marketing or background knowledge not confirmed in this pass.

## (A) Which mills outside China/India offer better quality-per-dollar or genuinely low MOQs for a small brand (tee 120–160 gsm quick-dry knit; short 110–145 gsm 4-way/mechanical-stretch woven)?

### Takeaway
Taiwan is the clearest "value step-up" tier: Far Eastern New Century (FENC) and Everest Textile supply Nike/Adidas/Lululemon/Patagonia-level brands, but neither publishes small-brand MOQs. The only Taiwan mill found with a documented low-MOQ route is Eclat Textile, via a Los Angeles stock programme plus knit-to-order from Taiwan, marketed to start-up brands; exact yardage minimums are not published. Sri Lanka (Hayleys Fabric, Teejay/Ocean Lanka) and Vietnam are large-volume weft-knit bases that serve big retailers, so a 300–1,000-unit brand is unlikely to get direct access. Turkey's low-MOQ offer is mostly at the garment-maker (CMT/private label) level, not the performance-fabric mill level.

### Inferences
- **Best candidate for the tee: Eclat's LA stock programme.** It is the only confirmed route where a Lululemon-tier Taiwan knitter openly targets start-ups with low MOQ, and it is Bluesign-certified.
  - Practical issues for a UAE brand: the stock is held in the US, and freight and duty to the UAE come on top.
  - The US representative appears to be "Allmore Group" / SoCal Textile Group. This is inferred from the listing names and should be confirmed.
- **FENC, Everest and Singtex are tier-1 quality but tier-1 MOQ.** Expect to reach them only through:
  - a garment factory that already buys from them (piggy-backing on its fabric orders),
  - a converter or agent, or
  - trade-fair sampling.
  Direct development orders at 300–1,000 garments are unlikely to be accepted. This is an inference: no MOQ is published.
- **Rough fabric volume for 300 tees.** Assuming ~0.25–0.3 kg of 150 gsm fabric per tee including waste, 300 tees need ~75–90 kg.
  - That is well below even China's "low" 300–500 kg per colour, so any direct mill programme will probably require stock/greige-dye programmes or one-colour runs.
  - 1,000 tees (~250–300 kg) approaches the bottom of a mill's per-colour MOQ.
- **Sri Lanka and Vietnam fit later scale-up, not a first run.** Hayleys, Teejay and Ocean Lanka run thousands of tonnes per month for big retailers. Vietnam's premium activewear garment MOQs run from 500–3,000 pieces per style.
- **Turkey looks like a garment-maker route, not a fabric-mill route.** The low MOQs found (100 units) are for CMT/private label using stock fabric. That route is credible for 300-unit runs and is close to the UAE, but fabric quality depends on what the maker stocks.
- **Price tier vs China.** No source gave per-metre prices for these mills. Industry convention (UNVERIFIED) puts Taiwan performance knits above mainland China and below Italian/Japanese premium mills (Eurojersey, Toray). Treat this as an unconfirmed expectation that needs quotes.

### Gaps

## (B) What are the practical low-MOQ routes for a small brand to get premium performance fabric?

### Takeaway
Several routes work at 300–1,000 garments.
- **SwatchOn:** 1-yard sample minimum and per-item wholesale minimums.
- **Mill Direct Textiles:** genuine Polartec with no minimums. Swatches cost $0.50; there is a 2-yard sample minimum and a $1,000 minimum for international full-roll orders.
- **The LYCRA Company's online COOLMAX sourcing hub:** links brands to certified mills.
- **Eclat's LA stock programme:** see (A).
- **Trade fairs:** Performance Days Munich (17–18 Mar and 27–28 Oct 2027) and Première Vision Paris (26–28 Jan 2027).

UAE textile traders in Deira and Dubai Textile City exist, but what they publicly list is mainly fashion wovens, not technical performance fabric.

### Cited Findings

**SwatchOn**
- How it works: "Check a fabric with its swatch book, order sample yardage from 1 yard, and order the same fabric again when it sells." — [SwatchOn – How it works](https://www.swatchon.com/how-it-works)
- Minimums: "Sample yardage starts at 1 yard on every fabric. Wholesale minimums are set per item — the item's price table shows its minimum and every price step above it." — [SwatchOn – How it works](https://www.swatchon.com/how-it-works)
- Pricing: "Prices are tiered per item: sample quantities cost more per yard than wholesale, and the per-yard price steps down as the quantity passes each threshold." — [SwatchOn – Fabric suppliers with no minimum](https://www.swatchon.com/fabric-suppliers-no-minimum)
- Reorders: catalog colours ship from stock and can be ordered again. — [SwatchOn – Fabric suppliers with no minimum](https://www.swatchon.com/fabric-suppliers-no-minimum)
- Swatch books contain one fabric's available colours as cuttings of about 10 × 10 cm. — [SwatchOn – Swatch books and credits](https://www.swatchon.com/help/articles/swatch-books-and-credits)
- Swatch credits:
  - Most books cost 1–3 credits, and shipping costs 3 credits per swatch order.
  - "Business accounts receive 20 credits once the Company Information form is submitted. Individual signup does not include welcome credits."
  - If the balance is short, the missing credits can be paid by card.
  - Swatch books arrive in 3–5 business days.
  — [SwatchOn – Swatch books and credits](https://www.swatchon.com/help/articles/swatch-books-and-credits)
- "Shipping, duties and available payment methods are confirmed during checkout." — [SwatchOn – How SWATCHON works (help)](https://www.swatchon.com/help/articles/how-swatchon-works)
- Unlisted fabrics can be found via keywords, filters, image search or a "sourcing request". — [SwatchOn Help Center](https://www.swatchon.com/help)

**Polartec via Mill Direct Textiles**
- "Our exclusive relationship with Polartec gives our customers the opportunity to purchase Polartec fabrics without minimums. We meet Polartec minimums…" — [Mill Direct Textiles](https://www.milldirecttextiles.com/)

  - "There is a 2 yard minimum for sample yardage", supplied via sister company Millyardage.com.
  - "There is a $1000 minimum order for Full Roll orders shipping outside of the USA… Shipping terms are XWORKS" (apparently Ex Works). International orders go through customerservice@milldirecttextiles.com, and freight takes about a week to arrange.
  — [Mill Direct – Ordering Information](https://www.milldirecttextiles.com/i/ordering-information)
- Polartec itself profiles Mill Direct as the route for "small-run quantities of Polartec fabric", including a case where a maker needed "small quantity orders and a few rolls here and there… without minimums". — [Polartec – Meet the Makers](https://www.polartec.com/news/meet-the-makers); [Polartec x Mill Direct](https://www.polartec.com/news/polartec-x-mill-direct-textiles-featured-partner)

### Inferences
- **Recommended sequence for a first run**
  1. Request SwatchOn swatch books (use the 20 business credits) and Mill Direct swatches to benchmark hand-feel cheaply.
  2. Request Eclat stock-programme headers.
  3. Visit Performance Days in March 2027, or use its year-round online product search, to meet Taiwanese, Korean and Turkish mills.
  4. Place 300–1,000-unit orders through SwatchOn wholesale tiers, Eclat stock or a garment maker's stock fabric. Defer custom development with FENC/Everest-tier mills until volumes reach mill per-colour MOQs.
- **Polartec via Mill Direct is the "real benchmark" route, not a cheaper one.** Its US prices plus the $1,000 international minimum and Ex Works terms mean it will not be cheaper than the benchmark. It does let the brand use genuine Polartec at small volumes if catalog fit is confirmed.
- **The COOLMAX hub is the most practical ingredient-brand route.** It points the brand to authorised mills, and labelling rights depend on buying from them. The fabric MOQ is still set by the mill.
- **Dubai/Sharjah traders are useful for speed and local trims, unlikely for technical knits.** Their public listings are fashion wovens (crepes, chiffon, rayon), so they are unlikely to stock Primeflex-class stretch wovens or quick-dry tee knits.
