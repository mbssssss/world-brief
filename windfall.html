Spend the Windfall

You just came into serious money. Pick how much (up to AED 100 million), then go shopping: supercars, mansions, first-class trips, watches, yachts, art, index funds, Bitcoin, charity. When you're done, fast-forward ten years and see what's left.

Prices are real

Approximate 2025–26 retail or market prices, in UAE dirhams at the AED 3.67 per US dollar peg.

Things cost money to own

Yachts, jets and mansions carry yearly upkeep. Cars lose value. Some watches, homes and art gain it.

Ten years later

Markets swing year by year. You get a net worth, a Lifestyle score and a Legacy score.
